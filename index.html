We make six beers.

Four stay the same, two change with the season. Every one of them is brewed twenty feet from the tap that pours it.

4.8% Pale Citra and Mosaic, finished dry.
4.2% Lager Helles. Four weeks in tank, nowhere to hide.
4.1% Bitter Best bitter on the hand pull. The regulars' pick.
4.6% Stout Oat stout. Black, smooth, not sweet.

The taproom.

Long benches, the tanks through the glass, and a food truck in the yard most weekends. No bookings, just come.

If beer is not your thing, the fridge has proper soft drinks and an alcohol-free version of the pale.
